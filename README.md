NapTrack — the worst app idea ever pitched, delivered with a straight face. One sentence, 225 characters, pitching an app that wakes you up every twenty minutes during your nap to confirm you are still napping, then grades your rest, ranks it against your neighbours, and emails you a certificate proving you rested efficiently. A product that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, and a function nobody on earth needs.
